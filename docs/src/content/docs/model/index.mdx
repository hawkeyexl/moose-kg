If you already maintain a controlled vocabulary, this track is about expressing it in the files
and getting it enforced. It assumes you know SKOS and does not explain it.

## What dockg models

| Your concern | dockg's surface | Emitted as |
|---|---|---|
| What a document is about | `prefLabel`, `altLabels`, `broader`, `narrower`, `related` | SKOS concepts and relationships |
| What kind of topic it is | `topicType` | `iirds:has-topic-type`, pointing at a published iiRDS individual |
| Which products it covers | `appliesTo`, `notApplicableTo` | `iirds:relates-to-product-variant`, `dockg:notApplicableToVariant` |
| Where in the lifecycle it sits | `softwareLifecyclePhase` | `iirds:relates-to-product-lifecycle-phase` |
| Which aspect it addresses | `softwareSubject`, `notSoftwareSubject` | `iirds:has-subject`, `dockg:notSoftwareSubject` |
| Which part of a document a claim covers | `kg.sections` | Per-section nodes carrying their own metadata |

<Aside type="note" title="dockg references iiRDS; it does not vendor it">
  Topic types, lifecycle phases, and subjects are bound to IRIs published by the iiRDS consortium.
  dockg never copies, re-serializes, or modifies the vocabulary — the IRIs in the source are
  verified against the published models. iiRDS is CC BY-ND, and referencing rather than
  redistributing is what keeps that obligation off your users.
</Aside>

## What it deliberately does not model

- **A concept scheme you did not declare.** Concepts are minted from the labels your documents
  use, converging on slugified labels. dockg will not invent a hierarchy for you.
- **Inference.** No transitive closure is materialized. `broader` chains are checked for cycles
  but not expanded — what you assert is what the graph contains.
- **Anything about your prose.** See [an index, not a corpus](/dockg/concepts/index-not-corpus/).

## Read this first if you are scoping by product

[Open-world semantics](/dockg/concepts/open-world/) explains what dockg assumes when a value is
absent. Getting that wrong produces scoped queries that are silently wrong in the dangerous
direction, and it is not recoverable by better tagging afterwards.
